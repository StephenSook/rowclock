MIT licence. They are copied verbatim from:

  PADS: Parkinson's Disease Smartwatch dataset, version 1.0.0
  PhysioNet, published 2024-03-25
  https://physionet.org/content/parkinsons-disease-smartwatch/1.0.0/

  Varghese J, Brenner A, Fujarski M, van Alen C, Plagwitz L, Warnecke T.
  Machine Learning in the Parkinson's disease smartwatch (PADS) dataset.
  npj Parkinsons Disease, 2024.

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

They are redistributed here under that licence, with attribution, for a
non-commercial student project, and any derivative of them carries the same
terms. They are included rather than fetched at runtime so that the live page
works with no network call and so a reader can verify our numbers against the
original files without downloading the full 469-participant corpus.

These are recordings of real people with real diagnoses. Nothing in this
directory is synthetic, and nothing in this project infers a diagnosis: the
condition labels come from the dataset and are reproduced, never predicted.
